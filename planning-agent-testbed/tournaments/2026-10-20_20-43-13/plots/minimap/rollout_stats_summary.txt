============================================================

A turn is one search (agent, map, opponent, player, game, round). States sampled = sum of
rollout_depth_reached over the turn's rollouts. Reward sd = standard deviation of the
rollout rewards within a turn (lower = cleaner reward signal; compare agents with the
same reward scale).
